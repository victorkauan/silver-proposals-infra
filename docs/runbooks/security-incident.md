# Respond to a personal data security incident

The on-call operator starts the incident record when a report or an [alarm](../reference/monitoring.md) indicates compromise. Record times with their time zone, affected environments, observed symptoms, and the time the controller learned that personal data was affected. Notify the operator via `privacidade@silversoft.com.br`.

1. Triage and contain. Check CloudWatch, WAF metrics, ECS and CloudTrail using time windows and request IDs. Restrict evidence access to responders. Revoke affected sessions or share links and isolate compromised workloads as needed. Record changes and preserve original logs or snapshots under restricted access.
2. Identify affected data subjects and data categories, quantities, duration, protective measures and likely harm. Evaluate relevant risk or damage, including sensitive data, minors, financial data, authentication data, protected data, and large-scale data. For customer proposal data handled as processor, inform the customer controller without undue delay and provide the information they need to assess notification.
3. When acting as controller, notify ANPD and affected people within three business days from knowing that the incident affected personal data if the relevant-risk threshold is met, subject to any applicable specific deadline. Submit through ANPD's official channel. Use clear language for people affected, covering the nature of the data, likely effects, mitigation and contact details. When information is incomplete, follow up with ANPD within 20 business days from the initial communication. Record the notification timestamps or the reason for the decision when notification is unnecessary.
4. Restore service, validate access control and affected data, and document follow-up owners and dates. Retain the incident record for at least five years, including incidents that did not require notification. Keep the record separate from routine application telemetry.

Sources for the controller's obligations and notification channel. [ANPD incident guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/comunicado-de-incidente-de-seguranca-cis), [Resolução CD/ANPD 15/2024](https://bibliotecadigital.mj.gov.br/bitstream/1/12879/2/RES_ANPD_2024_15.html).
